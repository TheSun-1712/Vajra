# Problem Statement & Implementation Progress Tracker

Turbulence in the atmosphere distorts a plane-parallel wavefront propagating through it. A Shack-Hartmann Wavefront Sensor (SH-WFS) samples this distorted wavefront using an array of small lenslets (the Microlens Array/MLA), which creates a spot-field on the detector. The deviation of these spots from their reference positions is used to derive the reconstructed wavefront and its associated Zernike coefficients. The conjugate of this reconstructed wavefront is used to derive an actuator command map, in units of actuator stroke length, which is fed to a Deformable Mirror (DM) to correct the distortion in real time.

This pipeline — **spot detection → centroid/deviation measurement → wavefront/Zernike reconstruction → conjugation → DM command → correction** — is the backbone of every SH-WFS AO system, including what NLST will need. Each stage of this pipeline has specific, well-documented failure modes. VAJRA addresses every stage.

Below, problems are organised by **which stage of the pipeline** they break, along with their current handling status in the project.

---

## STAGE 1: Spot Detection on the Detector

### Problem 1 — Spot detection on an extended source (the Sun) [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Centroiding is trivial for point-source stars (COM), but for the Sun, each lenslet subaperture images a patch of solar granulation—a textured, evolving scene with no single peak.
*   **Solution:** A CNN with spatial attention ingests the raw MLA detector frame and regresses Zernike coefficients directly, skipping the centroid/cross-correlation step entirely.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/reconstructor.py` (via `Layer0AttentionCNN`).

### Problem 2 — Spot detection under low photon flux [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Faint guide sources or partial cloud dropindividual subaperture SNR, injecting random noise into Zernikes.
*   **Solution:** Compute a per-subaperture confidence score from photon counts, measured spot FWHM, and local wavefront gradients to perform confidence-weighted least squares.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/detector.py` (via confidence estimation parameters) and `vajra/reconstructor.py` (via weighted least squares).

---

## STAGE 2: Deviation Measurement → Wavefront/Zernike Reconstruction

### Problem 3 — Centroid gain error / spot truncation [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Under strong seeing, spots shift into neighbouring boxes or elongate, causing systematic centroid gain compression.
*   **Solution:** The direct image-to-Zernike CNN implicitly bypasses WFS centroiding, eliminating centroid-gain compression.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/reconstructor.py`.

### Problem 4 — Zernike modal aliasing / fitting error [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Spatial frequencies higher than MLA sampling can resolve alias into lower-order modes.
*   **Solution:** Geometrically restrict Zernike mode counts according to lenslet sampling cutoff, and penalize high-order assigning during training.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/config.py` (`ZERNIKE_MODES_MAX = 66`) and `train_end_to_end.py`.

### Problem 5 — Non-uniform turbulence across the aperture (spatial gain) [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Standard modal gain optimization uses a single gain for the entire aperture, failing when turbulence is concentrated in part of the pupil.
*   **Solution:** Maintain a live gain value per subaperture updated from WFS SNR metrics, and apply it directly in the slope-to-command path.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (via `modal_gains` and spatial gain parameters).

---

## STAGE 3: Reconstructed Wavefront → Conjugate → Actuator Map

### Problem 6 — Geometry mismatch between WFS grid and DM actuator grid [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Discrepancy between WFS lenslet grid and DM actuator grids drifts due to mechanical wear/hysteresis.
*   **Solution:** Influence function checks monitor localized response discrepancies and update the reconstruction matrices.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/calibration.py` (via `InfluenceFunctionCalibrator`).

### Problem 7 — Actuator stroke saturation during conjugation [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Converting conjugate wavefronts into stroke commands exceeds actuator bounds.
*   **Solution:** A deep neural network is trained offline to compute stroke-constrained command mappings in microseconds.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (via `SaturationNN`).

## STAGE 4: System-Level / Temporal Loop Issues

### Problem 8 — Latency between measurement and actuation (the lag problem) [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Measurement and actuation delay (1-2 ms) causes corrections to be out of sync with wind-shear transitions.
*   **Solution:** Classify turbulence regimes (Steady / Transitioning / Broken) via spatial correlation structures and blend predictor models.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (via `RegimeAwarePredictor` and `RecurrentWavefrontPredictor` GRU forecasting).

### Problem 9 — Drift of the interaction/reconstruction matrix [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Gravity-vector changes and thermal expansion slowly invalidate WFS-to-DM calibration matrices.
*   **Solution:** Monitor correlations between actuator commands and residual errors to trigger targeted re-pokes.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/calibration.py` (via `LoopCalibrationMonitor`).

### Problem 10 — Non-common-path aberrations (NCPA) [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Imperfections downstream of WFS splitter are invisible to the loop, causing science image speckles.
*   **Solution:** Slow background estimator infers the NCPA phase screen from science focal plane speckles.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/science.py` (via `NCPATracker`).

### Problem 11 — Reduced field of view / isoplanatism [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Turbulence correction is only strictly valid along WFS line of sight, degrading at off-axis points.
*   **Solution:** Separate ground-layer component (common across field) from high-altitude component (path-dependent).
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (via `GLAODecomposer`).

### Problem 12 — Single-conjugate mirror physical constraint [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** A single DM cannot physically hold different shapes for different paths.
*   **Solution:** Rapidly alternate DM between virtual conjugate states within a single camera exposure.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (via `TemporalMultiplexor`).

### Problem 13 — Wavelength mismatch between WFS and science paths [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** WFS senses at different bands than science camera, introducing chromatic scaling errors.
*   **Solution:** Compute predictive chromatic corrections based on scaling laws and airmass angles.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (via `ChromaticOffsetModel`).

### Problem 14 — Mechanical mount vibrations [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Cooling pumps and wind shake inject narrow-band mechanical vibrations.
*   **Solution:** Feed-forward double-integrate mount acceleration sensors and apply active notch filtering.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (via `MechanicalNotchFilter` and `RegimeAwarePredictor` mount inputs).

### Problem 16 — Telemetry-driven PSF reconstruction [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Post-processing requires accurate maps of the time-varying, off-axis PSF.
*   **Solution:** Save reduced-rate loop telemetry to parameterize a physical PSF reconstruction model.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/science.py` (via `PSFReconstructor`).

### Problem 18 — Scintillation errors [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Local near-ground turbulence causes intensity dropouts, corrupting standard phase measurements.
*   **Solution:** Track per-subaperture flux and down-weight centroid contributions during scintillation.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/detector.py`.

### Problem 19 — MLA-to-detector registration drift [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Temperature variations shift MLA pixels relative to CCD grids.
*   **Solution:** Monitor long-term common-mode spot drift and calibrate zero-points dynamically.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `run_vajra.py` (via `registration_offset` tracking).

### Problem 20 — Loop timing jitter [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** OS scheduling and bus latencies introduce varying loop time gaps.
*   **Solution:** Timestamp frames at capture and feed exact loop delays directly to predictors.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `run_vajra.py` (via `dt_latency` prediction).

### Problem 21 — DM hysteresis and creep [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Piezoceramic stroke outputs deviate from requested shapes based on voltage histories.
*   **Solution:** Implement inverse pre-shaping on commanded actuator voltage signals.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py`.

### Problem 22 — Partial pupil illumination / vignetting [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Cloud cover or edge vignetting breaks static pupil assumptions.
*   **Solution:** Dynamically mask and exclude dark subapertures from reconstruction matrices.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/reconstructor.py`.

## ADVANCED OPTIMIZATIONS ADDED

### 1. Model Quantization (INT8) [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** FP32 deep networks are computationally demanding for 1 kHz loops.
*   **Solution:** Dynamically quantize Linear/GRU layers to INT8.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `run_vajra.py` (reducing sizes from 0.82 MB to 0.63 MB, 1.3x smaller).

### 2. Recurrent Zernike Predictor (GRU) [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Linear predictors struggle to model non-stationary, non-linear atmospheric boil.
*   **Solution:** Run a recurrent GRU network on Zernike sequences.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `vajra/controller.py` (`RecurrentWavefrontPredictor`).

### 3. Self-Supervised Solar Contrastive Pre-training [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Models trained on static solar scenes fail to generalize to new granulation textures.
*   **Solution:** Pre-train ResNet backbone with SimCLR contrastive loss using rot/trans/brightness scales.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `train_end_to_end.py`.

### 4. Multi-Rate Loop Control [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Reconstructing high-order Zernikes is slow, whereas 90% of seeing noise lies in fast tip-tilt.
*   **Solution:** Run Tip-Tilt mirror corrections at 1 kHz and Deformable Mirror high-order updates at 500 Hz.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `run_vajra.py`.

### 5. Differentiable End-to-End Loop Training [HANDLED]
*   **Mechanism:** Decoupled models do not optimize globally for specific DM geometries.
*   **Solution:** Train WFS and DM controller networks jointly by backpropagating loss through a Fourier optics propagator.
*   **Status:** **[FULLY IMPLEMENTED]** in `train_end_to_end.py`.

## NEW UNEXPLORED STELLAR PROBLEMS

### Problem 26 — Coronagraph Speckles (Non-Common Path Aberrations - NCPA)
*   **Mechanism:** Slowly drifting mechanical/thermal aberrations downstream of WFS splitter create quasi-static speckles on the science focal plane, obscuring faint exoplanets.
*   **Proposed Solution:** Implement active Speckle Nulling directly on the science camera. Inject phase-shifting probes onto the DM to measure the complex electric field of the speckles and apply destructive cancellation commands.

### Problem 27 — Strong Scintillation (Phase-Amplitude Coupling)
*   **Mechanism:** In bad seeing, pupil intensity fluctuations (scintillation) violate phase-only WFS assumptions.
*   **Proposed Solution:** A Dual-DM architecture where a pupil-conjugated DM corrects phase aberrations and a second, high-altitude-conjugated DM corrects amplitude scintillation.

### Problem 28 — Sodium Laser Guide Star (LGS) Cone Effect
*   **Mechanism:** Sodium guide stars reside at ~90 km altitude. The WFS measures a conical path rather than a cylinder, leaving high-altitude turbulence uncorrected (focus anisoplanatism).
*   **Proposed Solution:** Volumetric Tomographic Reconstruction. Project a constellation of LGS and tomographically reconstruct the 3D atmosphere volume using a 3D Kalman filter or 3D CNN.

### Problem 29 — Actuator Hysteresis RNN Compensator
*   **Mechanism:** Piezoceramic DM actuators exhibit significant non-linear hysteresis (10-15% of stroke limit), adding non-linear temporal lag.
*   **Proposed Solution:** Build a Recurrent Neural Network (LSTM or GRU) that runs inline to model the inverse hysteresis operator, outputting pre-distorted voltages.

### Problem 30 — Drifting Structural Vibrations and Resonances
*   **Mechanism:** Narrow-band mechanical vibrations (e.g. wind-shake, cryocooler compressions) split and shift frequencies as the telescope changes pointing elevation, rendering static notch filters ineffective.
*   **Proposed Solution:** A Linear Quadratic Gaussian (LQG) controller tracking structural resonance parameters from WFS centroids dynamically using an Adaptive Kalman Frequency Tracker.
